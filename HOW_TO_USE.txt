HAND CONTROL
Control your laptop with hand gestures, using the webcam.

START
  Double-click HandControl.exe. A window opens showing your camera with
  the hand skeleton drawn on your hand.

  The first time, Windows may show "Windows protected your PC".
  Click "More info", then "Run anyway". (The app is not signed by a
  company, so Windows does not recognise it yet.)

GESTURES
  Thumbs up ................. volume up    (keeps going while you hold it)
  Thumbs down ............... volume down  (keeps going while you hold it)
  Point right (index) ....... Right arrow
  Point left (index) ........ Left arrow
  Pinch (thumb + index) ..... Space bar    (one press per pinch)

  Hold a gesture for a moment before it fires. Keep your hand upright
  or level: diagonal gestures are ignored on purpose.

GOOD TO KNOW
  - Arrow keys and space go to the window you clicked last. Click your
    slides, video or photo viewer once after starting Hand Control.
  - In the Hand Control window: P pauses, Q or Esc quits.
  - If it says the camera cannot be opened, close other apps that use
    the camera (Zoom, Teams, the Camera app) and try again.
  - Good light helps. Keep your whole hand inside the picture.

PRIVACY
  The camera picture is processed on your computer only. Nothing is
  recorded, saved or sent anywhere, and the app does not use the internet.

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and a webcam are required.
